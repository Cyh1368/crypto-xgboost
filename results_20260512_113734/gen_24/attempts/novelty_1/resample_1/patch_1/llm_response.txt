<DESCRIPTION>
This edit targets the two main failure modes: prediction collapse and regime fragility. I reduce over-regularization slightly so XGBoost can express more slope, while keeping depth moderate to avoid overfit. On the feature side, I add more regime-invariant short-horizon signals: rolling z-scored volatility, rolling z-scored trend, and a compact volatility-adjusted momentum interaction. These are designed to survive the train→test→val regime shift better than absolute-level indicators. The new features are all causal and keep the total feature count safely below the 80-feature limit.
</DESCRIPTION>
