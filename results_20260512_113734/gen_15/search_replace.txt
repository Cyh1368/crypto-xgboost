The current model is severely over-regularized, causing predictions to collapse toward the mean (pred_std_ratio=0.035, train_slope=0.0076). The primary issue is excessive regularization combined with insufficient model capacity.

Analysis of the problem:
1. The model has max_depth=3, min_child_weight=10, reg_lambda=8.0, reg_alpha=1.5, and gamma=0.2 - all extremely conservative
2. This heavy regularization is destroying the model's ability to learn even the training patterns
3. The slope collapse (val_slope=0.0016, train_slope=0.0076) indicates the model cannot express directional predictions
4. The good news: generalization_ratio=85.4% means what little the model learns does generalize well

Strategy:
1. Significantly increase model expressivity by reducing regularization and increasing depth
2. Add more estimators but with a higher learning rate to maintain training speed
3. Keep min_child_weight moderate to preserve some generalization
4. The features are already well-designed with z-score normalization and short lookbacks, so we mainly need to let the model use them

Expected outcome:
- Train slope should increase from 0.0076 to at least 0.020 (3x improvement)
- Pred_std_ratio should increase from 0.035 to at least 0.10 (3x improvement)
- With the strong feature engineering already in place, the generalization ratio should remain good (>60%)
- Target: val_slope >= 0.020, val_DA >= 0.520
