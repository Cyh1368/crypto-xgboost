<DESCRIPTION>
The current program achieves a strong 96.0% generalization ratio (val_r/test_r), indicating that the rolling z-score normalization at [10, 20, 40] windows is working well for regime invariance. However, the 19.9% slope ratio (val_slope/train_slope) shows that the model still struggles to transfer predictive patterns to the validation period.

The key insight is that while z-scores make features regime-invariant at individual timescales, we need to explicitly capture when SHORT-TERM behavior diverges from LONG-TERM behavior - these divergences signal regime transitions that are predictive across periods.

This edit:
1. Extends the multi-scale z-score normalization to include 60 and 80-bar windows for enhanced long-term regime awareness
2. Creates explicit regime shift indicators: `regime_shift_short_long = ret_z_20 - ret_z_60` and `regime_shift_medium_long = ret_z_40 - ret_z_80`
3. Removes the least important time-of-day features (`hour_sin`, `hour_cos`, `dow_sin`, `dow_cos`) to stay within the 80-feature limit while adding more predictive regime indicators

The regime shift indicators measure when normalized short/medium-term momentum deviates from normalized long-term momentum, which is a transferable signal that works across different market regimes. This should improve val_slope while maintaining the strong generalization ratio.
</DESCRIPTION>
