<DESCRIPTION>
This edit focuses on the root failure mode: the model’s slope collapses in the newest regime and predictions are too mean-reverting. I’m making the feature space more regime-invariant by emphasizing short windows and rolling z-normalized transforms, while also adding explicit “change in market character” features such as volatility trend, autocorrelation trend, and efficiency trend. These are more likely to survive a second regime shift than absolute levels.

On the model side, I slightly increase expressivity by reducing regularization and tree constraints a bit, while keeping the ensemble size moderate to avoid overfitting. The goal is to raise pred_std_ratio and val slope without losing the current acceptable directional accuracy. The feature count remains safely below 80.
</DESCRIPTION>
