<DESCRIPTION>
This edit introduces several refinements to the signal generation logic:
1.  **Enhanced Microstructure Quality:** Incorporates `obi_tau10` and re-weights the `micro_quality` metric to give more importance to `book_pressure`, which is often a more stable indicator of liquidity flow.
2.  **Price Exhaustion Filters:** Adds `vwap_dev` and `rsi_14` filters to the entry logic to prevent entering trades at local price extremes (e.g., buying the top of a move).
3.  **Liquidity-Aware Sizing:** Uses `kyle_lambda_est` to apply a size penalty when market impact is expected to be high, protecting against slippage.
4.  **Volatility-Adaptive Exits:** Scales `take_profit`, `stop_loss`, and `max_bars` based on the `vol_regime`. In high-volatility environments, targets and stops are widened to avoid noise, and the holding time is reduced. In low-volatility environments, targets are tightened and the holding time is extended to allow moves to develop.
5.  **Tighter Entry Constraints:** Slightly tightens the `funding`, `spread_bps`, and `micro_quality` thresholds to improve the precision of entries.
