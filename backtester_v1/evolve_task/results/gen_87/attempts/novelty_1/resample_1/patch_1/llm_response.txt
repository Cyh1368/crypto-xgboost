<DESCRIPTION>
This update enhances the signal generation logic by incorporating trend-adaptive thresholding and a mean-reversion filter. 
1. Trend-Adaptive Thresholding: We introduce `trend_strength` to adjust the entry threshold, allowing the model to be more sensitive during strong trends (a technique that historically improved profit by increasing trade count in momentum phases).
2. OBI Expansion: Incorporates `obi_tau10` into the `micro_quality` metric for a more holistic view of the order book.
3. Mean-Reversion Guard: Utilizes `vwap_dev` to filter out entries when the price is significantly far from its VWAP, reducing the risk of buying local tops or selling local bottoms.
4. Threshold & Filter Refinement: Base thresholds and microstructure filters are slightly tightened to prioritize signal quality.
