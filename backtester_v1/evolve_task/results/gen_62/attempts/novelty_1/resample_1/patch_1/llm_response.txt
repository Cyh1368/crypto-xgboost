<DESCRIPTION>
This update significantly increases trade frequency and capital efficiency by adopting the aggressive thresholding and microstructure filtering logic that powered the highest-performing historical iteration (90.84 score). 

Key changes:
1.  **Lowered Entry Thresholds**: Reduced the base threshold from 0.0019 to 0.0016 and relaxed session-specific hurdles to capture more alpha-rich opportunities.
2.  **Relaxed Microstructure Filters**: Expanded the OBI and VWAP deviation limits (e.g., OBI from 0.5 to 0.75, VWAP dev from 0.01 to 0.025) to prevent over-filtering in high-conviction regimes.
3.  **Simplified Risk Targets**: Shifted from complex ATR-based TP/SL to robust, near-fixed targets (0.0042/0.003) which have shown superior stability in backtests.
4.  **Aggressive Sizing**: Increased the base position size to 0.14 while maintaining inverse-volatility scaling to protect the Sharpe ratio during high-volatility periods.
5.  **Liquidity Safety**: Added a spread-based filter (spread_bps < 10.0) to avoid entering trades during toxic liquidity conditions.
