<DESCRIPTION>
The current program is overly conservative, as evidenced by its high profit factor (55.13) and win rate (0.84) but low trade count (150) compared to the top-performing seed (462 trades). This edit increases trade frequency and total return by:
1. Lowering the entry threshold from a liquidity-penalized ~0.0025-0.003 down to a fixed 0.0018.
2. Replacing the restrictive RSI limits with looser filters (82/18) and incorporating Order Book Imbalance (OBI) to ensure microstructure alignment.
3. Increasing the base position size from 0.10 to 0.12 and capping it at 0.18.
4. Anchoring the dynamic TP/SL floors to the seed's 0.004/0.003 while retaining ATR-based scaling for volatility regimes.
5. Slightly lowering the trend strength requirement for hold-time extensions to capture more momentum moves.
