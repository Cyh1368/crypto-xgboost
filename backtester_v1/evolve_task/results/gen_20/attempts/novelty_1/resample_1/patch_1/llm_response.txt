# Analysis

Looking at the performance progression:
- **Baseline (simple)**: 80.50 (32.89 Sharpe, 15.29% return, 0.78 win rate, 462 trades)
- **Current best**: 87.56 (33.16 Sharpe, 21.28% return, 0.78 win rate, 468 trades)

The current program achieves excellent Sharpe (33.16) and win rate (0.78), but I notice:

1. **Max drawdown increased to 0.31** (from 0.25 in baseline) - this suggests we're taking larger losses or longer drawdown periods
2. **Return improved significantly to 21.28%** - but at the cost of higher drawdown
3. **Profit factor excellent at 18.54** - very few losing trades

The key insight: **We're being too aggressive with position sizing and not adjusting stops based on signal quality and microstructure conditions.**

## Proposed Improvements

I'll implement three targeted changes:

1. **Dynamic stop-loss based on volatility and microstructure quality** - tighter stops in high-vol/poor-quality environments
2. **Asymmetric position sizing** - larger positions for high-conviction signals with good microstructure, smaller for marginal signals
3. **RSI-based entry filter** - avoid overbought/oversold extremes which have lower edge
4. **Funding rate adjustment to position size** - reduce size when funding is extreme (indicating crowded trades)

## Rationale

1. **RSI Filter (6-period)**: Fast RSI extremes (>75 or <25) indicate overbought/oversold conditions with lower edge. This should reduce false signals and improve win rate without sacrificing too many trades.

2. **Signal Conviction Metric**: Measures predicted return relative to recent volatility. High conviction signals (where predicted return is large relative to vol) deserve larger positions.

3. **Dynamic Stop Loss**: 
   - Expands stops when volatility is spiking (vol5 > vol60) to avoid whipsaws
   - Tightens stops when microstructure is poor to limit losses in toxic flow
   - This should reduce max drawdown while maintaining win rate

4. **Improved Position Sizing**:
   - Bases on conviction, not just absolute return magnitude
   - Incorporates microstructure quality (better book = larger position)
   - Reduces size when funding is extreme (crowded trades)
   - Clamped range (6%-16%) prevents extreme positions

These changes target the **0.31 max drawdown** issue by being more defensive in poor conditions while maintaining aggression in high-quality setups.
