<DESCRIPTION>
This update introduces several targeted improvements to the signal generation and risk management layers:
1.  **Trend-Adaptive Thresholding**: Lowers the entry threshold during periods of high `trend_strength` to capture momentum more effectively, while increasing it when the price is significantly deviated from VWAP (`vwap_dev`) to avoid buying local tops or selling local bottoms.
2.  **Liquidity-Aware Filtering**: Incorporates `kyle_lambda_est` into the microstructure filter to avoid entries in high-impact/low-liquidity environments, which typically suffer from higher slippage and lower win rates.
3.  **Asymmetric Exit Strategy**: Implements directional asymmetry in TP/SL levels. Longs are given slightly wider targets and tighter stops, while shorts have tighter targets and wider stops to account for the typical "slow grind up, fast crash down" and short-squeeze dynamics of crypto markets.
4.  **Volatility-Trend Sizing**: Scales the position size based on the `vol_trend` (v5/v60), increasing capital allocation when short-term volatility is expanding relative to the long-term average.
5.  **Refined Microstructure Thresholds**: Slightly tightens the `micro_quality`, `funding_rate`, and `spread_bps` filters to prioritize trade quality and maintain the high win rate.
</DESCRIPTION>
