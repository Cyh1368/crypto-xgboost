- Current: Score 98.22 (slight regression from best)

**Key observations:**

1. The current program is nearly identical to Seed 3 but with slightly worse metrics (98.22 vs 98.36), suggesting small parameter tweaks or thresholding logic degradation.

2. **Win rate stability**: All high performers maintain 0.76 win rate, but current has more trades (535 vs 530), suggesting lower quality entries are being generated.

3. **Profit factor decay**: Current shows 15.13 vs 15.95 in Seed 2/3, indicating larger losing trades relative to winners.

4. **Microstructure opportunity**: The context provides rich signals (RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, momentum, skew, kurtosis, autocorrelation) that aren't being used for entry confirmation or volatility regime detection.

5. **Exit timing vulnerability**: Current uses only vol_trend for max_bars logic, missing potential mean-reversion signals from RSI/Bollinger Bands that could improve profit factor.

# Proposed Improvements

I'll focus on three targeted mutations:

1. **Enhanced Entry Confirmation**: Add RSI and Bollinger Band filters to reduce false signal generation (lower trade count while maintaining/improving win rate)
2. **Improved Volatility Regime Detection**: Use skew and kurtosis to better identify high-quality entry environments
3. **Dynamic Position Sizing Refinement**: Scale conviction bonus more aggressively in high-quality microstructure environments

These filters target the most common failure mode of pure threshold-based systems: entering into exhaustion patterns. By requiring both model prediction AND price action alignment, we reduce false signals while keeping the system responsive to genuine momentum shifts. The bounds are intentionally conservative (0.2-0.8 range) to avoid over-filtering and preserve trade frequency near 530.

This improves profit factor by eliminating low-quality marginal entries while maintaining win rate.

</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
The current funding filters use thresholds of ±0.0015 for applying the 0.90x penalty, which allows marginal positions to run at full size even with unfavorable funding costs.

This mutation tightens the funding filter thresholds from ±0.0015 to ±0.0010 and increases the penalty multiplier from 0.90 to 0.85 when funding is unfavorable. This better accounts for carry cost in extended positions, which is particularly important given the max_bars window of 3-4 bars (45-60 minutes).

For a position held 4 bars with adverse funding:
- Old: 0.90x penalty (10% reduction)
- New: 0.85x penalty (15% reduction)

This prevents the model from over-allocating capital to trades with adverse funding while preserving aggressiveness in funded carries. Expected effect: improved Sharpe ratio through better risk-adjusted sizing and reduced drawdowns from funding bleed.
</DESCRIPTION>
