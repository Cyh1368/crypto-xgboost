<DESCRIPTION>
This update introduces several refinements to improve signal quality and capture more profitable momentum trades:
1.  **Trend-Adaptive Thresholds**: Lowers the entry threshold during strong trends (using `trend_strength`) to capture momentum earlier, similar to the high-performing 98.52 version but with a more aggressive 5% adjustment.
2.  **Enhanced Microstructure Quality**: Incorporates `obi_tau10` into the `micro_quality` metric to provide a more stable view of order book imbalance across multiple time horizons.
3.  **Exhaustion & Mean-Reversion Filters**: Adds loose filters using `rsi_14` and `vwap_dev` to avoid entering long at extreme overbought levels or entering short at extreme oversold levels.
4.  **Dynamic Time Exits**: Extends the `max_bars` limit when volatility is trending up or when the price is significantly deviated from VWAP, allowing more time for the predicted move or mean reversion to materialize.
</DESCRIPTION>
