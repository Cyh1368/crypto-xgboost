## Analysis

The current implementation is very basic:
- Uses only `predicted_return` for entry decisions
- Fixed position sizing regardless of market conditions
- No volatility adjustment for stops/targets
- Ignores all the rich microstructure and price action data available
- No session-aware logic despite having session flags
- No funding rate consideration despite having it available

## Proposed Improvements

I'll implement three targeted mutations:

1. **Volatility-adjusted position sizing and stops** - Use vol_20 to scale position size and stops dynamically
2. **Microstructure-based entry filters** - Use order book imbalance and spread to confirm entries
3. **Session and funding-aware logic** - Adjust thresholds based on market session and funding rate
