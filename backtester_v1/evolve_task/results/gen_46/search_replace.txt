4.  **Sizing Adjustments**: Increased base position size slightly (0.13 to 0.14) and added a volatility-regime penalty to reduce exposure during periods of extreme instability.
5.  **Dynamic Time Horizon**: Allowed trending trades to stay open for 5 bars (vs 4) to capture more persistence when trend strength is high.
