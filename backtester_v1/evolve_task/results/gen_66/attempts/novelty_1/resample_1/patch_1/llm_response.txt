# Analysis

Looking at the performance metrics, the current program achieves a combined score of 98.27 with solid performance (Sharpe 34.35, win_rate 0.76). The prior iterations show similar performance, suggesting we've hit diminishing returns on the current approach.

Key observations:
1. **Win rate is high (0.76)** but we could improve profit factor (15.49) by being more selective on trades
2. **Max drawdown is 0.47**, which is reasonable but could be tightened with better exit logic
3. **Trade frequency (532 trades)** is healthy—we don't want to reduce this dramatically
4. **The elastic thresholding and conviction-based sizing are working well**

## Proposed Improvements

I'll implement three targeted mutations:

1. **Enhanced microstructure filtering during strong adverse conditions** - Use order book imbalance asymmetry and depth ratios to filter false breakouts in unfavorable market structure
2. **Dynamic stop-loss based on price action volatility (ATR)** rather than just vol_impulse - This adapts to actual realized volatility on the bar
3. **Add RSI extremes as a contrarian filter** - When RSI is extremely overbought/oversold with a positive signal (or vice versa), reduce conviction to avoid mean-reversion traps

These changes target improving the Calmar ratio by reducing drawdowns while maintaining win rate, and improving profit factor by being pickier about entries in unfavorable setups.
