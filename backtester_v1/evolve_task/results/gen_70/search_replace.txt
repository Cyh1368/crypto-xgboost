Optimizing the signal generator for a higher combined score by:

2. Introducing a wick exhaustion filter (wick_ratio_up/down) to avoid entering trades at local price extremes where reversal likelihood is higher.
3. Enhancements to the dynamic hold time (max_bars) by incorporating autocorrelation and lower trend thresholds.
4. Stabilizing position sizing (moving away from 0.19 max size to a more centered 0.14-0.16) and slightly tightening the stop loss (0.0030 -> 0.0029) to improve the Sharpe ratio and reduce max drawdown.
