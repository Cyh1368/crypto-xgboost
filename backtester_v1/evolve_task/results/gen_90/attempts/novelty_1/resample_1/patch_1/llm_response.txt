<DESCRIPTION>
This edit improves the signal generator by:
1.  **Session-Aware Entry Hurdles**: Implementing specific base hurdles for US (lower hurdle for higher efficiency) and Asia sessions (higher hurdle for slower/mean-reverting markets).
2.  **Robust Micro-Alignment**: Using an average of multiple OBI timescales (tau1, tau5, tau10) to create a more stable liquidity filter (obi_avg).
3.  **Aggressive Sizing**: Increasing the base position size and confidence scaling to capture more return, while maintaining a liquidity-based scaler (Kyle's Lambda) and an ATR-based volatility cap.
4.  **Optimized Risk Management**: Adjusting Take Profit (TP) and Stop Loss (SL) to slightly wider levels (0.0043/0.0032) and adding a trend-following TP bonus to capture momentum.
5.  **Refined Funding Filter**: Tightening the funding rate entry filter to avoid entering crowded trades during extreme funding periods.
