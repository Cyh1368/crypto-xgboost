<DESCRIPTION>
This edit aligns the signal generator with the highest-performing parameters identified in previous iterations while incorporating refined session-based and microstructure filters.

Key adjustments include:
1.  **Threshold Reset**: Standardize the entry threshold to 0.0018, which historically yielded the highest trade count and Sharpe.
2.  **Filter Expansion**: Loosened the OBI hurdle to -0.7/+0.7 and the spread limit to 10 bps to capture more alpha while maintaining essential floor protection.
3.  **Mean Reversion Filter**: Integrated `vwap_dev` (VWAP deviation) to prevent entry during extreme overextensions, which improves the win rate on mean-reverting signals.
4.  **Position Size Standardization**: Fixed the position size at 0.13, mirroring the best-performing regime's approach to capitalize on high win-rate signals.
5.  **Fixed Risk Targets**: Reverted to fixed 0.004/0.003 risk targets, which proved more robust than volatility-adjusted ones over the 15-minute horizon.
6.  **Context-Aware Hold Times**: Re-enabled the 5-bar hold extension for trending US sessions to maximize gain retention.
