4. **Asymmetric Hold Times**: The current max_bars logic is symmetric. We can extend holds for high-confidence trades or high-autocorrelation regimes.

5. **RSI Confirmation Filter**: Adding RSI extremes as a confirmation filter can reduce false signals in mean-reversion scenarios.
