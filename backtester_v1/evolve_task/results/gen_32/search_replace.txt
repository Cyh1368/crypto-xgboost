This update aims to push the performance closer to the high-Sharpe 'Seed' strategy by tightening entry thresholds and returning to the more robust risk management parameters (0.4% TP / 0.3% SL).

Key changes:
1.  **Raised Entry Thresholds**: Reverted focus to high-conviction predictions, using a range from 0.185% to 0.22% depending on the session (US vs. Asia) to improve trade quality and win rate.
2.  **Stricter Microstructure Filters**: Narrowed the Order Book Imbalance (OBI) requirement (-0.25 to 0.25) and added a `realized_vol_ratio` filter to bypass periods of excessive volatility where XGBoost predictions often degrade.
3.  **Liquidity Constraint**: Tightened the maximum allowable spread to 5 bps to ensure efficient execution.
4.  **Optimized Risk/Reward**: Realigned TP/SL to the Seed's 40bps/30bps values which demonstrated superior Sharpe and Drawdown performance in previous iterations.
