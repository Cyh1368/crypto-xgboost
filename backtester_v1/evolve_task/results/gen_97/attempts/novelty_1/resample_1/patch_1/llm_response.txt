<DESCRIPTION>
This edit upgrades the signal from a pure threshold-and-microstructure approach into a lightweight regime-aware selector.

The main improvement is adding a small set of orthogonal filters that are common sources of edge in short-horizon crypto:
- trend confirmation via `trend_strength`, `macd_signal`, `autocorr_5`, and recent returns,
- exhaustion/mean-reversion avoidance via `rsi_14`, `bb_pct`, and `vwap_dev`,
- execution quality via `depth_ratio_5`, `kyle_lambda_est`, and `spread_bps`,
- carry-risk timing via `funding_rate`, `funding_8h_ma`, and `minutes_to_funding`.

It keeps the original threshold structure and sizing logic intact, but makes entries more selective when the market is noisy or crowded, while allowing asymmetric long/short behavior when momentum and order flow align. Stops and take-profit are also made slightly more adaptive to ATR and microstructure friction, which should help preserve win rate while improving profitability per trade.
</DESCRIPTION>
