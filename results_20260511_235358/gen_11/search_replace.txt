<DESCRIPTION>
The model currently suffers from 'slope collapse' and poor generalization across the test-validation boundary, indicating that it is learning regime-specific patterns that do not hold in the most recent market data.

To address this, I am implementing the following strategies:
1. **Regime-Invariant Features**: Adding features that normalize price action and volatility against their own recent history. Specifically:
   - `vol_z_100`: Z-score of current volatility relative to the last 100 periods.
   - `mom_z_10`: Momentum (10-period return) scaled by the current volatility, making a 1% move in low-vol regimes comparable to a 1% move in high-vol regimes.
   - `price_z_40`: Z-score of price relative to its 40-period moving average and standard deviation.
   - `autocorr_10`: Rolling 10-period autocorrelation of returns to identify trending vs. mean-reverting regimes.
2. **Momentum-Efficiency Interaction**: Adding an interaction between momentum and market efficiency (Kaufman's Efficiency Ratio). Momentum is generally more reliable in high-efficiency (trending) regimes.
3. **Increased Regularization**: Reducing `max_depth` to 3 and significantly increasing `min_child_weight` and `reg_lambda`. This forces the XGBoost model to find broader, more robust patterns that are less likely to disappear during regime shifts.
4. **Slower Learning**: Reducing the `learning_rate` and `n_estimators` to prevent the model from over-fitting the training regime's noise.
</DESCRIPTION>
