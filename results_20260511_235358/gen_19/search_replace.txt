<DESCRIPTION>
The primary issue is a massive slope collapse (0.07 -> 0.006) and a Directional Accuracy drop (0.57 -> 0.51) between training and validation. This indicates the model is learning regime-specific magnitudes and patterns that disappear in the validation period.

To fix this, I am:
1.  **Transforming features to be regime-invariant:** Applying rolling Z-scores to returns, volatility, and volume. This ensures the model sees a "high vol" or "high return" signal relative to the current local market context rather than absolute values.
2.  **Improving Generalization via XGBoost Hyperparams:**
    - Reducing `max_depth` to 4 to prevent complex overfitting.
    - Increasing `min_child_weight` to 20 to ensure patterns are supported by a larger sample size.
    - Adding L2 regularization (`reg_lambda=10.0`) to promote weight stability across regimes.
    - Lowering the `learning_rate` to 0.07 for more conservative updates.
3.  **Refining interactions:** Using volatility-normalized momentum (Sharpe-style) which is more robust than simple multiplication.
</DESCRIPTION>
