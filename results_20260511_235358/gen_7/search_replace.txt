<DESCRIPTION>
The primary issue is a massive slope collapse (0.074 to 0.006) and prediction compression (std ratio 0.11), indicating the model is over-relying on absolute price moves and volatility levels that shifted between the training and validation periods.

1.  **Hyperparameters**: Reduced `max_depth` to 4 and substantially increased `min_child_weight` to 20. This prevents the model from splitting on small clusters of noise, forcing it to find features that carry weight across large portions of the data. Increased `reg_lambda` to 10.0 to further dampen outlier sensitivity.
2.  **Z-Score Normalization**: Replaced absolute historical returns with rolling Z-scores (period 20 and 60). This transforms "a 0.5% move" into "a +2.0 sigma move," which generalizes significantly better across high and low volatility regimes.
3.  **Volatility Ratio**: Switched from absolute volatility to volatility decay/expansion (fast/slow ratios), which is more indicative of regime changes than absolute log-returns.
4.  **Shorter Lookbacks**: Removed long-term ranking (200-bar) which often acts as a lagging indicator of past regimes.
5.  **Target Clipping**: Kept clipping at 98% but ensured the model has enough expressivity through a lower learning rate (0.05) and more estimators to find the signal.
</DESCRIPTION>
