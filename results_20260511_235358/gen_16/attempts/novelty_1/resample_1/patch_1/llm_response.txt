**Rationale for these changes:**

1. **Shorter lookback windows (5-10 vs 20-60)**: Features decay faster and adapt to regime shifts. A 5-bar volatility captures immediate market character, not long-term average.

4. **Increased estimators (400→600)**: More capacity to learn the subtle variance in predictions while short lookbacks provide better inductive bias.

5. **Slight regularization increase (lambda 0→0.5)** with higher learning rate reduction: Prevents overfitting to transient noise in the validation regime.

6. **Simplified, normalized interactions**: `momentum_vol_prod` is normalized by rolling mean vol, so it's regime-invariant.
