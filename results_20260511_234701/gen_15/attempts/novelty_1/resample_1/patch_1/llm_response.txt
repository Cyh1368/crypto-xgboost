<DESCRIPTION>
The current model suffers from a severe predictive slope collapse (0.0017 on validation), indicating that it learns patterns that do not generalize across market regimes. 

To fix this, I am implementing the following changes:
1. **Hyperparameter Robustness**: Reduced `max_depth` to 4 and increased `min_child_weight` to 5. This forces the model to learn from larger, more robust samples and prevents overfitting to regime-specific noise. I also added L1/L2 regularization (`reg_alpha`, `reg_lambda`) and slowed the `learning_rate` to 0.07.
2. **Z-Score Normalization**: Added Z-scored volatility and volume features. Normalizing these by their own rolling mean/std makes them regime-invariant (e.g., a "high volume" period is defined relative to the recent past, not an absolute level).
3. **Kaufman Efficiency Ratio**: Added Efficiency Ratio (ER) for multiple windows. ER measures the "trendiness" of price action (net move vs. total path), which is a highly robust indicator of market character across regimes.
4. **Volatility-Adjusted Momentum**: Added a momentum feature scaled by its own realized volatility, ensuring the signal strength is consistent regardless of the volatility regime.
5. **Target Clipping**: Adjusted `CLIP_PERCENTILE` to 97 to reduce the influence of extreme outliers that rarely repeat across different market periods.
</DESCRIPTION>
