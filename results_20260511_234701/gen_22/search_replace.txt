The model is currently suffering from severe prediction collapse (val_slope=0.0006, pred_std_ratio=0.0146), indicating it is over-regularized and under-expressive.
1. Reduced `min_child_weight` (20 -> 5) and `reg_lambda` (10.0 -> 1.0) to allow the model to make bolder predictions and increase the predictive slope.
2. Increased `max_depth` (4 -> 5) and `learning_rate` (0.05 -> 0.07) to increase model expressivity and capture more complex patterns.
3. Shortened the Z-score normalization window (100 -> 60) to make features more adaptive to recent market regimes, which is critical for generalizing across the regime shifts observed in the val period.
4. Added more return lags [2, 8] and a `vol_trend` feature to capture the rate of change in market character.
5. Modified the momentum-volatility interaction to be multiplicative with the volatility regime, which often generalizes better than simple division.
