<DESCRIPTION>
The primary issue is a severe slope collapse (0.074 to 0.006) and directional accuracy degradation between training and validation sets, indicating the model is learning regime-specific patterns that do not generalize. 

To solve this, I am:
1.  **Normalizing features via rolling Z-scores**: This makes features like returns, volume, and volatility regime-invariant. A 1% move is normalized by the current volatility, ensuring the model interprets it consistently across high and low vol periods.
2.  **Regularizing XGBoost**: Increasing `min_child_weight` from 1 to 20 and reducing `max_depth` from 5 to 4 forces the model to learn only the most robust, high-sample patterns.
3.  **Slowing down learning**: Reducing the `learning_rate` to 0.05 and increasing `n_estimators` allows for more stable convergence.
4.  **Adding regime-invariant features**: Introducing "Volatility Trend" (short-term vs long-term vol) and "Distance from High/Low" to capture market character regardless of absolute price levels.
5.  **Clipping targets more aggressively**: Reducing `CLIP_PERCENTILE` to 95 to focus the model on the predictable bulk of the distribution rather than outliers.
</DESCRIPTION>
