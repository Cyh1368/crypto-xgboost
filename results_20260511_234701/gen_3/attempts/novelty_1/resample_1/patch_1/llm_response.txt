<DESCRIPTION>
This edit addresses the primary problem of slope collapse (0.074 -> 0.006) and the sharp degradation from test to validation. 

1.  **Regime-Invariant Features:** Absolute returns and volatility levels are replaced with rolling Z-scores and volatility-normalized returns. This ensures that a "1% move" or "high volume" is interpreted relative to the recent context (e.g., last 100-200 bars) rather than the global training mean, which is crucial for generalizing across different market regimes (2023 vs 2024).
2.  **Shorter Lookbacks:** Reduced the maximum lookback for primary features from 60+ to 48 bars to capture faster-decaying but more robust patterns.
3.  **Increased Regularization:** 
    - Decreased `max_depth` to 3 and significantly increased `min_child_weight` to 20 to prevent the model from learning noise-driven splits.
    - Increased `reg_lambda` to 10.0 to penalize large weights.
    - Lowered `learning_rate` to 0.05 for more stable convergence.
4.  **Target Metrics:** These changes aim to boost `val_slope` by making features more consistent across splits and `val_DA` by focusing on robust, high-sample patterns.
</DESCRIPTION>
