The primary problem is slope collapse (val_slope=0.006 vs train_slope=0.074), indicating the model learns regime-specific absolute patterns that fail in the validation period. 

This approach solves the problem by:
1.  **Strict Regime Invariance:** Every feature is transformed into a rolling z-score or a relative ratio (e.g., price position in range, efficiency ratio). This ensures that a "large move" or "high volatility" is defined relative to the *recent* market character (last 10-60 bars) rather than absolute historical levels.
2.  **Short-Horizon Focus:** Lookbacks are capped at 60 bars (15 hours) to ensure the model adapts quickly to regime shifts.
3.  **Robust Regularization:** Increased `min_child_weight` (30) and `reg_lambda` (2.0) force the model to learn patterns that are consistent across large samples, preventing it from fitting to noise in the training set.
4.  **Interaction Terms:** Multiplicative terms like `momentum * volatility_regime` are explicitly provided to help the model distinguish between trend-following and mean-reverting environments.
5.  **Target Robustness:** Clipping targets at the 97th percentile and using a conservative learning rate (0.04) prevents outliers from distorting the predictive slope.
