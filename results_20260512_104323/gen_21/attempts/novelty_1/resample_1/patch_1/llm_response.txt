<DESCRIPTION>
The model currently suffers from prediction collapse (low pred_std_ratio) and a near-zero predictive slope on the validation set, indicating it's too conservative and failing to generalize across regime shifts. 

I am proposing three main changes:
1. **Increase Model Expressivity**: Raising `max_depth` to 5, increasing `n_estimators` to 450, and reducing `min_child_weight` and `reg_lambda`. This directly addresses the "collapsed toward mean" warning by allowing the model to capture more complex, high-variance patterns.
2. **Regime-Invariant Character Features**: Adding higher-order moments (skewness, kurtosis) and "volatility of volatility" (VVoV) to help the model identify market regimes (e.g., trending vs. mean-reverting, stable vs. unstable) that are invariant to absolute price levels.
3. **Risk-Adjusted Momentum**: Adding a Sharpe-like feature (momentum divided by volatility) and range expansion metrics to better normalize the "signal" across different volatility environments.
4. **Target & Variance Adjustments**: Reducing `CLIP_PERCENTILE` to 97 to focus on the core distribution and increasing `MIN_PRED_STD_RATIO` to 0.22 to force the model to produce more distinct predictions.
</DESCRIPTION>
