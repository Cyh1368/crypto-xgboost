<DESCRIPTION>
The current model shows a significant improvement in `pred_std_ratio` (0.0322 vs 0.0063 in previous iterations) but suffers from low validation directional accuracy (`val_DA=0.5025`). This suggests that while the added features (candle structure, z-scores) are providing more expressivity, the model is overfitting to noise or failing to capture broader market regimes.

I am proposing the following changes:
1. **Hyperparameter Tuning**: Increase `max_depth` to 6 and `learning_rate` to 0.05 to maintain expressivity, while significantly increasing `min_child_weight` (7) and `reg_lambda` (1.5) to improve generalization and validation performance.
2. **Target Clipping**: Reduce `CLIP_PERCENTILE` to 95. This helps the model focus on the core distribution of returns rather than being skewed by extreme outliers, which often improves directional accuracy.
3. **Structural Features**: Add features that capture the price's relationship to long-term averages and trend efficiency:
    - `ema_20_dist` & `ema_100_dist`: Distance from short and medium-term EMAs to capture mean-reversion potential.
    - `efficiency_ratio_14`: Kaufman's Efficiency Ratio to distinguish between trending and range-bound markets.
    - `adx_14`: A simplified Average Directional Index to quantify trend strength.
    - `price_speed_3`: ATR-normalized price velocity.
