The previous model is suffering from a significant "prediction collapse" (pred_std_ratio = 0.04, well below the target 0.15), meaning it is essentially predicting the mean return due to a low signal-to-noise ratio in the provided features. To combat this, I am:
1. Refining the feature set to include 'Efficiency Ratio' (to distinguish trend from noise), 'BB Width' (to capture volatility clusters), and 'Range Stretch' (to identify overextended price action).
2. Adjusting hyperparameters: lowering 'max_depth' to 7 to improve generalization (deep trees often fit noise in 15m bars) and slowing the 'learning_rate' while increasing the number of estimators and patience. This encourages the model to find subtle directional signals that are otherwise lost in MSE optimization.
3. Increasing 'CLIP_PERCENTILE' to 99 to preserve more target variance, which can help the model distinguish between meaningful moves and noise.
4. Adding interactions between volume, volatility, and price direction to provide more 'expressivity' to the trees.
