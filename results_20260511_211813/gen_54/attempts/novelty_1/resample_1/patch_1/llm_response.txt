The current model suffers from a severe prediction collapse (pred_std_ratio = 0.03), indicating it is too regularized or lacks sufficient signal to make bold directional bets. 

To address this, I am:
1.  **Increasing Model Expressivity**: Significantly increasing the `learning_rate` (0.03 -> 0.18) and removing all L1/L2 regularization (`reg_alpha`, `reg_lambda` -> 0). I'm also setting `min_child_weight` to 1 to allow the model to capture more specific market states.
2.  **Adding Volatility and Momentum Acceleration Features**: Introducing features that capture regime shifts and "shocks," such as `vol_expansion`, `volume_shock`, and `price_accel`. These features help the model identify turning points and high-conviction moves.
3.  **Refining Normalization**: Adding `vwap_dev_std` (VWAP deviation normalized by rolling volatility) and `range_pos_10` to provide better context for mean reversion vs. trend continuation.
4.  **Adjusting Clipping**: Reducing `CLIP_PERCENTILE` to 98 to help the model focus on the more frequent, tradable part of the return distribution.
