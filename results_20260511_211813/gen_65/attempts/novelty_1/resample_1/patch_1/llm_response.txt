<DESCRIPTION>
This edit aims to address the "predictions collapsed toward mean" warning by increasing model expressivity and adding higher-resolution features. 
1.  **Hyperparameters**: Increased `max_depth` to 6, `learning_rate` to 0.05, and `n_estimators` to 1000, while reducing regularization (`reg_lambda` and `min_child_weight`). This allows the model to capture more complex patterns and move away from the mean.
2.  **Volatility Features**: Added `vol_of_vol_24` and `skew_20` to capture the stability and asymmetry of the volatility regime.
3.  **Range Features**: Added `dist_max_48` and `dist_min_48` to provide the model with context regarding the current price's position relative to the 12-hour high/low.
4.  **Volume Features**: Added `log_vol` to capture the raw scale of activity, which can be useful for regime identification.
5.  **Interaction Features**: Added `vol_weighted_mom` to combine price momentum with relative volume intensity.
6.  **Optimization**: Replaced the `apply`-based OBI calculation with a faster list comprehension to ensure execution stays within the 3-minute limit.
