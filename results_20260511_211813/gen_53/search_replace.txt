## Root Cause Analysis

The problem is **excessive regularization and insufficient signal capture**:
- `reg_lambda` values (0.01-0.5) are too high for sparse market signals
- Low `learning_rate` (0.02-0.05) combined with high regularization suppresses feature importance
- The model is **over-regularized relative to the signal-to-noise ratio** in 15-min perp futures
- Features are good but the model can't express them effectively

## Solution Strategy

1. **Dramatically reduce regularization** (reg_lambda → 0, reg_alpha → 0) to allow model expressivity
2. **Increase learning_rate** to 0.15 for faster convergence and stronger signals
3. **Reduce n_estimators** to avoid overfitting with low regularization
4. **Adjust max_depth to 5** for a sweet spot: deep enough for interactions, shallow enough to avoid noise
5. **Tighten early_stopping** to prevent training artifacts when using low regularization
6. **Remove weakest features** (vol_zscore_100, rolling_sharpe_14, momentum_consistency_12) that may be adding noise rather than signal

The strategy is: **less regularization + faster learning + fewer but stronger features = higher pred_std_ratio and better generalization**.
