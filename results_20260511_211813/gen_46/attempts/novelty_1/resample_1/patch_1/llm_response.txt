<DESCRIPTION>
This implementation addresses the "prediction collapse" issue by significantly increasing model expressivity and introducing features that capture structural market shifts. 

Key innovations:
1. **Expressivity Boost**: Reduced `min_child_weight` to 2 and `reg_lambda` to 0.1, while increasing `max_depth` to 6. This forces the model to capture more granular signals rather than defaulting to the mean.
2. **Structural Divergence Features**: Added "Efficiency Ratio" (Kaufman) and "Volatility of Volatility" to distinguish between trending and noisy regimes.
3. **Relative State Normalization**: Implemented rolling Z-scores and ranks for returns and volume to make the model invariant to absolute price levels while sensitive to local anomalies.
4. **Microstructure Dynamics**: Introduced "Price Velocity" and "Volume Force" to capture the momentum of capital flow rather than just price action.
5. **Target Sensitivity**: Adjusted `CLIP_PERCENTILE` to 95 to focus the model on the most predictable part of the return distribution, reducing the impact of fat-tailed noise that often causes XGBoost to collapse toward zero.
