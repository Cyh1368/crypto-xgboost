<DESCRIPTION>
The current model's performance drop (from -0.15 to -0.57) is primarily due to an extra `shift(1)` applied to the base OHLCV series, which makes the features 1-bar stale relative to the target. I am reverting this shift to align with the successful prior programs.

Additionally, the model is suffering from "prediction collapse" (pred_std_ratio = 0.01), meaning it is underfitting and predicting the mean. I am increasing model expressivity by:
1. Increasing `max_depth` and `learning_rate`.
2. Reducing `reg_lambda` to allow the model to capture more signal.
3. Adding high-signal features like `vol_cv_20`, `dist_h_100`, and `force_index_7`.
4. Removing redundant feature definitions and the arbitrary `iloc[:, :80]` cap to ensure all signal-carrying features (like funding and orderbook) are preserved.
</DESCRIPTION>
