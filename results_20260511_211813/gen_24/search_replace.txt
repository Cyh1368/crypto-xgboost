The current model is suffering from a "collapsed toward mean" issue (pred_std_ratio = 0.0139), which indicates it is being too conservative in its predictions. To address this, I am:
1. Increasing model expressivity by increasing `n_estimators` to 800 and `max_depth` to 7, while lowering `learning_rate` to 0.01 for more stable convergence.
2. Reducing regularization (`min_child_weight` to 1, `reg_alpha` to 0.0, and `reg_lambda` to 0.05) to allow the model to fit more complex patterns.
3. Enriching the feature set with micro-structure indicators (candle wicks, body size), price location within recent ranges (`range_pos_20`), volume z-scores, and local return autocorrelation to provide more granular signals.
4. Adding a funding rate change feature to capture shifts in market sentiment.
