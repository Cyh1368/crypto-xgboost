<DESCRIPTION>
To address the "collapsed toward mean" prediction issue (low pred_std_ratio), I am increasing model expressivity by increasing `max_depth` to 7 and reducing regularization parameters (`min_child_weight` to 1, `reg_alpha` to 0.0, and `reg_lambda` to 0.05). 

I am also adding several high-signal features to capture market efficiency and microstructure better:
1. **Kaufman Efficiency Ratio (ER)**: To distinguish between noisy mean-reverting phases and clean trending phases.
2. **ATR-normalized volatility and returns**: To help the model better scale its predictions relative to current market volatility.
3. **Volatility-adjusted returns**: Improving the signal-to-noise ratio of input returns.
4. **Range expansion & Z-distance to EMA**: To capture local exhaustion and breakout signatures.

`CLIP_PERCENTILE` is lowered to 95 to reduce the impact of noisy outliers in the 15-minute crypto return distribution, which can often cause regression models to shrink towards zero.
</DESCRIPTION>
