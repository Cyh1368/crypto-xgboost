<DESCRIPTION>
The previous model suffered from a significant prediction collapse (pred_std_ratio = 0.027), indicating it was underfitting the variance of the target. To counter this, I am dramatically increasing the model capacity by raising n_estimators and max_depth, while simultaneously lowering the learning rate to ensure stable convergence. I also increased CLIP_PERCENTILE to 98th percentile to retain more tail signals.

On the feature engineering side, I've added:
1. Autoregressive features: Lagged returns (t-1, t-2) to capture short-term momentum or mean-reversion persistence.
2. Distributional features: Rolling skewness (24h) to identify asymmetrical risk regimes.
3. Lagged exogenous features: Lagged Order Book Imbalance (OBI) and Funding Rate to better capture lead-lag relationships which are often present in perp futures.
These additions stay within the 80-feature limit while providing the model with more context to avoid simply predicting the mean.
