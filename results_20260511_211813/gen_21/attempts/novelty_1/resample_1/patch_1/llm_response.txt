<DESCRIPTION>
The previous model showed good directional accuracy (0.53+) but suffered from a significant "collapse" penalty, meaning the predictions were too clustered around the mean. To address this, I am:
1. Increasing model expressivity by increasing `max_depth` to 7 and `n_estimators` to 1000, while reducing `reg_lambda` and `reg_alpha` to allow the model to capture more granular signals.
2. Adjusting `CLIP_PERCENTILE` to 98 to preserve more target variance, which provides stronger gradients for the model to learn from and encourages higher prediction dispersion.
3. Adding several high-value features:
   - `dist_ema_200`: Long-term trend deviation.
   - `vpt_20`: Volume-Price Trend to capture liquidity-driven moves.
   - `vol_skew_30`: Rolling skewness of returns to identify asymmetric risk regimes.
   - `vol_shock_5_20`: Short-term volatility spikes relative to the medium-term.
   - `ny_session`: A binary indicator for the high-volatility US trading window.
   - `volume_z_60`: Z-scored volume over a longer lookback to identify significant flow.
These changes aim to push the `pred_std_ratio` closer to the 0.15 target while maintaining or improving DA.
</DESCRIPTION>
