<DESCRIPTION>
The current model is overfitting (Train DA 0.59 vs Val DA 0.51) and its predictions are collapsing toward the mean (pred_std_ratio 0.08). To address this, I am proposing a shallower model (max_depth 5) with a slower learning rate (0.02) and increased regularization (reg_alpha 0.05, reg_lambda 0.5). This should improve generalization. 

Additionally, I am adding several high-signal features to help the model distinguish between different market regimes and identify mean-reversion opportunities:
1. ADX (Average Directional Index): To measure trend strength.
2. Choppiness Index: To distinguish between trending and ranging markets.
3. VWAP Deviation normalized by ATR: A more robust mean-reversion signal.
4. Price-Volume Correlation: To detect accumulation/distribution phases.
5. Distance from 20-period High/Low: To identify potential trend exhaustion.
6. 20-period Volume Z-Score: To capture short-term volume spikes.

These changes aim to increase the model's confidence on unseen data, thereby improving both directional accuracy and the prediction standard deviation ratio.
