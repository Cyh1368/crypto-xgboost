<DESCRIPTION>
The current model suffers from a significant 'prediction collapse' (pred_std_ratio = 0.04), meaning it is underfitting the variance of the target returns. To address this, I am increasing the model's expressivity by:
1. Increasing `max_depth` from 5 to 7 and decreasing `min_child_weight` from 3 to 1 to allow the trees to capture more granular patterns.
2. Reducing `reg_lambda` from 0.6 to 0.1 to loosen the L2 penalty on leaf weights.
3. Increasing `learning_rate` to 0.05 to allow the model to learn faster and produce more distinct predictions.
4. Adding high-signal features: Rolling Autocorrelation (to capture momentum/mean-reversion persistence), Efficiency Ratio (to distinguish between trending and range-bound markets), and Volatility Shocks.
5. Pruning redundant lags to stay within the 80-feature limit and avoid the curse of dimensionality.
