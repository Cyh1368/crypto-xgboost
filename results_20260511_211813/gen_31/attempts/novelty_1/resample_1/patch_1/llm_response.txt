<DESCRIPTION>
The current model is still collapsing toward the mean, which usually means the trees are too constrained and the feature space does not provide enough separable structure. I would address this in two complementary ways:

1. Loosen the XGBoost bias slightly:
   - reduce `min_child_weight` and regularization,
   - increase tree count a bit,
   - use a slightly higher learning rate with more expressive trees.
   This should increase prediction variance without pushing into heavy overfit, since the current train/val gap is small.

2. Add a small set of regime-conditioned lagged features:
   - short-horizon return and volatility ratios,
   - volatility-adjusted momentum,
   - trend slope proxy,
   - rolling correlation between volume and returns,
   - candlestick body/wick structure,
   - normalized mean-reversion distance and range position.
   These are all past-only and help the model distinguish when momentum vs reversion is more likely. The feature count remains comfortably below the 80-feature limit.

I am also slightly tightening the target clipping from 99 to 98.5 to reduce extreme-label noise while keeping enough signal.
</DESCRIPTION>
