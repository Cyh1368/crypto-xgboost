<DESCRIPTION>
The model is currently suffering from prediction collapse (pred_std_ratio < 0.15), meaning it is too conservative and predicts values close to the mean. To address this, I am proposing several changes:
1. **Increase Model Expressivity**: Increased `max_depth` to 8 and `learning_rate` to 0.07, while removing `reg_lambda` entirely. This forces the model to fit the variance in the data more aggressively.
2. **Loosen Target Clipping**: Increased `CLIP_PERCENTILE` to 99.5. This allows the model to see more of the tail moves, which often contain the strongest directional signals.
3. **Microstructure Features**: Added several new features to capture price action dynamics:
    - `bb_width` and `vol_sqz`: To identify volatility cycles and breakouts.
    - `vpt` (Volume-Price Trend): To capture the strength of price moves confirmed by volume.
    - `tail_ratio`: To identify candle-level exhaustion or rejection.
    - `directional_intensity`: To measure the 'cleanliness' of a move over 5 bars.
    - `vwap_dev_atr`: To normalize price deviations from VWAP by recent volatility.
    - `vol_of_vol`: To capture regime shifts in volatility itself.
4. **Regularization Tuning**: Added a small `gamma` (0.01) to ensure that while the model is more expressive, it only splits when there is a tangible gain, helping to manage the increased depth.
</DESCRIPTION>
