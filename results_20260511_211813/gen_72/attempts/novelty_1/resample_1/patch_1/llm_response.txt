The model currently suffers from 'prediction collapse' (pred_std_ratio = 0.056, target 0.15), meaning it is under-fitting the signal or overly regularized. 

I am proposing two main changes:
1.  **Hyperparameter Adjustment**: Increase `max_depth` to 7 and `n_estimators` to 1000 to increase model expressivity. Simultaneously, I am reducing `reg_alpha` and `reg_lambda` to allow the model to capture more variance in the features. I've also increased `CLIP_PERCENTILE` to 99.0 to preserve more signal in the tails of the return distribution.
2.  **Feature Engineering**: Added several high-value regime and momentum features:
    *   **Efficiency Ratio (Kaufman)**: Measures the 'trendiness' of price action vs noise.
    *   **Trend Acceleration**: Captures changes in momentum (momentum of momentum).
    *   **Volatility of Volatility**: A robust regime indicator for identifying shifting market conditions.
    *   **Range Z-Score**: Normalizes the current True Range to detect volatility expansion/compression.
    *   **Z-Close**: Measures the distance of the current price from its moving average, normalized by volatility (mean-reversion indicator).
    *   **Trend-Volatility Interaction**: Helps the model distinguish between high-volatility trends and high-volatility mean-reversion.
