<DESCRIPTION>
The previous model suffered from 'mean prediction collapse' as indicated by the very low pred_std_ratio (0.0091 vs goal of 0.15) and text feedback. This suggests the model was too heavily regularized and lacking in expressivity to capture subtle signals in noisy 15-minute crypto futures data.

I am proposing the following changes:
1. **Hyperparameters**: Significantly increase the learning rate (0.01 -> 0.05), increase tree depth (4 -> 7), and reduce the minimum child weight (10 -> 2). This allows the trees to find more specific, smaller-scale patterns. I also reduced reg_alpha and reg_lambda to loosen the regularization penalty.
2. **Feature Engineering**: Re-introduced and expanded the signal set to include:
   - **Momentum Acceleration**: Captures the second derivative of price movement.
   - **Mean Reversion/Relative Position**: Price distance from 30-bar High/Low and 10/50 SMA ratios.
   - **Candle Shadows**: Capture intra-bar volatility and potential rejection candles.
   - **Volatility Expansion**: Short-term vs long-term volatility ratios to identify breakouts.
   - **Volume Z-Score**: Identifies unusual participation compared to a trailing window.

These changes are designed to push the model's predictions away from the mean and closer to the actual return standard deviation while maintaining directional accuracy.
</DESCRIPTION>
