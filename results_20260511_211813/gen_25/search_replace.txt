<DESCRIPTION>
The previous model suffered from a high 'collapse penalty' (pred_std_ratio = 0.026, far below the 0.15 threshold), meaning its predictions were too conservative and shrunk toward the mean.

This proposal:
1. Significanty reduces regularization (min_child_weight, reg_alpha, reg_lambda) and increases the learning rate (0.015 -> 0.05) to increase model expressivity and force a higher prediction variance.
2. Increases CLIP_PERCENTILE from 95 to 98 to retain more information from the tails of the distribution.
3. Adds 'momentum persistence' (streak_5) and 'vwap distance' normalized by ATR to provide clearer mean-reversion and trend-continuation signals.
4. Optimizes the OBI calculation to avoid slow row-wise apply() calls, ensuring execution time stays within limits.
</DESCRIPTION>
