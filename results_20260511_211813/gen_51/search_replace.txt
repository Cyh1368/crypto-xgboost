<DESCRIPTION>
The previous model suffered from a "collapsed toward mean" warning (pred_std_ratio ~0.08), indicating it was too conservative. To address this, I am increasing the model's expressivity by raising `max_depth` to 9 and increasing the `learning_rate` to 0.05.

To manage the resulting risk of overfitting (the overfit gap was already 0.07), I am:
1. Increasing regularization (`reg_lambda` and `reg_alpha`).
2. Reducing `subsample` and `colsample_bytree` to 0.6 to introduce more stochasticity and prevent reliance on specific features.
3. Adding more robust statistical features (skewness, kurtosis, volatility-of-volatility, and rolling Sharpe ratio) to help the model identify different market regimes.
4. Adding price-action features like price-volume divergence and distance from 24-hour highs/lows to provide better anchor points for directional moves.
5. Lowering `CLIP_PERCENTILE` to 96 to reduce the denominator in the `pred_std_ratio` calculation by excluding extreme outliers that the model cannot realistically predict.
</DESCRIPTION>
