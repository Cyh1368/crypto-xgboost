<DESCRIPTION>
The previous model suffered from a significant 'prediction collapse' (pred_std_ratio ~0.04), indicating it was overly regularized or lacked enough expressivity to distinguish signal from noise in 15-minute crypto data.

I am making the following improvements:
1. **Model Expressivity**: Increased `max_depth` to 6, `learning_rate` to 0.05, and decreased `min_child_weight` to 1. Reduced `reg_lambda` to 0.2 to allow the model to capture more variance, directly addressing the collapse warning.
2. **Feature Selection & Microstructure**: I pruned redundant return and momentum horizons to stay under the 80-feature limit, ensuring that high-value features (Funding Rate, Orderbook Imbalance, and Regime Interactions) aren't truncated by the `iloc[:, :80]` safety catch.
3. **New Features**:
   - Added **Choppiness Index**: To help the model ignore low-signal sideways periods.
   - Added **Price-Volume Pressure**: To capture divergences between price action and participation.
   - Added **Efficiency Ratio**: Kaufman's metric for trend quality.
4. **Targeting Consistency**: Adjusted `CLIP_PERCENTILE` to 99.0 to preserve more extreme signals during training, which can help increase the standard deviation of predictions.
</DESCRIPTION>
