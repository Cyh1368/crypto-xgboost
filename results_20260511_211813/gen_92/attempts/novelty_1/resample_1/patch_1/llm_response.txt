<DESCRIPTION>
This algorithm addresses the "prediction collapse" issue by significantly increasing model expressivity and introducing regime-invariant features. Key innovations include:
1. **Z-Score Normalization**: Momentum and volatility features are normalized by their rolling distributions, allowing the model to identify relative signal strength regardless of the current market volatility regime.
2. **Microstructure Price Action**: Features like upper/lower wick ratios and body-to-ATR ratios capture candle-level exhaustion and momentum signals often missed by simple returns.
3. **Donchian-Style Positioning**: Distance from 24-hour highs and lows provides context for mean reversion vs. breakout regimes.
4. **Expressive XGB Configuration**: By reducing regularization (reg_alpha/lambda) and increasing the learning rate and depth, the model is forced to "commit" to directional predictions, increasing the prediction standard deviation ratio and reducing the collapse penalty.
5. **Momentum Persistence**: A rolling autocorrelation proxy identifies whether the current market is in a trending or mean-reverting state.
