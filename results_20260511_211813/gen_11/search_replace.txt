<DESCRIPTION>
The previous model was severely underfitting ('collapsed toward mean'), likely due to overly aggressive regularization and low learning rate.

1. Hyperparameter Tuning: I increased the learning rate (0.01 -> 0.05) and max_depth (4 -> 6) while significantly lowering the min_child_weight and regularization parameters (reg_alpha, reg_lambda). This aims to increase the 'pred_std_ratio' above the 0.15 threshold and allow the model to capture more signal.
2. Feature Engineering: Added mean-reversion signals (SMA deviations, price position in recent 20-bar range), volatility-normalized range (HL range), and momentum acceleration (RSI diff). These provide additional directional context that was missing in the 'current' version but is critical for 15-minute timeframe directional accuracy.
