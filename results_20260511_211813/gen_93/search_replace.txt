The model currently suffers from prediction collapse toward the mean (pred_std_ratio < 0.15), hindering its ability to capture directional momentum.

This proposal:
1. Increases model expressivity by increasing 'max_depth' to 6 and 'n_estimators' to 800, while lowering the 'learning_rate' to 0.08 to ensure more stable convergence.
2. Introduces 'reg_lambda' of 0.05 to gently manage the increased complexity.
3. Adds 'rolling_sharpe_14', 'vol_shock_5_40', and 'vol_zscore_20' to the feature set. These provide the model with a clear sense of risk-adjusted returns and volume surges, which are critical for avoiding mean-collapse during directional moves.
4. Adds 'momentum_consistency' to help the model distinguish between noisy range-bound motion and trending regimes.
