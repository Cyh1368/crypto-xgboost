<DESCRIPTION>
This algorithm implements a novel approach centered on "Multi-Horizon Efficiency and Volatility-Normalized Momentum." Drawing inspiration from Kaufman's Efficiency Ratio (ER), it distinguishes between "clean" trending moves and "noisy" mean-reverting regimes. Key innovations include:
1. **Efficiency-Weighted Momentum**: Interacting Kaufman's ER with multi-horizon returns to identify high-conviction trend continuations.
2. **Volatility-Adjusted Momentum (VAM)**: Normalizing returns by rolling volatility to create a Sharpe-like signal per bar, reducing noise in high-volatility regimes.
3. **Microstructure Divergence**: Using Orderbook Imbalance (OBI) and its rate of change, interacted with trend strength, to capture leading signals of price exhaustion.
4. **Expressive Hyperparameters**: To combat the "collapse toward mean" warning, I've significantly reduced regularization (reg_lambda, reg_alpha) and increased the learning rate and model depth, forcing the model to be more "opinionated" while maintaining generalization through subsampling.
5. **Regime-Conditioned Interactions**: Explicitly modeling the interaction between volatility regimes, trend efficiency, and candle anatomy to capture non-linear market dynamics.
