<DESCRIPTION>
The current model suffers from a severe 'prediction collapse' (pred_std_ratio = 0.0166), meaning it is predicting values very close to the mean and failing to capture the variance of returns. To address this, I am proposing two main changes:

1.  **Increase Model Expressivity**: I am significantly loosening the XGBoost hyperparameters. By increasing `max_depth` to 8, decreasing `min_child_weight` to 1, and reducing regularization (`reg_alpha` and `reg_lambda`), the model will be more capable of fitting the complex, non-linear relationships in the data. I've also increased the `learning_rate` to 0.1 to help the model move away from the mean-prediction bias.
2.  **Enhanced Feature Engineering**: I am adding several statistical and momentum features to provide better signals for directional movement:
    *   **Z-scores**: Rolling Z-scores for price and volume to identify mean-reversion opportunities.
    *   **Higher-order Moments**: Rolling skewness and kurtosis to capture the 'shape' of recent return distributions and tail risks.
    *   **Stochastic Oscillator**: A classic momentum indicator to identify overbought/oversold conditions.
    *   **Momentum Acceleration**: Capturing the change in momentum (ret_1 - ret_3).
    *   **Funding Velocity**: Capturing the rate of change in funding rates, which often precedes price shifts in perp markets.
</DESCRIPTION>
