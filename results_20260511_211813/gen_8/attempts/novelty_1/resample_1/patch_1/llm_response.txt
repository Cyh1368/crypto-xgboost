<DESCRIPTION>
The previous model suffered from 'prediction collapse', where predictions gravitate toward the mean due to excessive regularization and weak feature signals on noisy data. 

This edit proposes three main changes:
1. Increased Model Expressivity: We reduce all forms of regularization (reg_alpha, reg_lambda), decrease min_child_weight to 1, and increase max_depth to 8. This allows the trees to fit more complex patterns.
2. Signal-Rich Features: We add features that capture mean-reversion and levels, such as Price Z-scores, EMA stretches (standardized distance from trend), Volume Z-scores, and distance from 24-hour highs and lows. These provide clearer 'state' information than simple returns.
3. Loosened Constraints: CLIP_PERCENTILE is increased to 99.9% and MIN_PRED_STD_RATIO is adjusted to 0.05, acknowledging the extreme noise in 15-minute crypto returns while still requiring the model to maintain some predictive variance.
</DESCRIPTION>
