<DESCRIPTION>
The model is currently suffering from "mean collapse," where predictions are too close to zero (pred_std_ratio ~0.05, while the target is >0.15). This is caused by excessive regularization or insufficient model expressivity relative to the noise in the target.

I am proposing the following changes:
1.  **Hyperparameters**: Increase `max_depth` to 7 and `n_estimators` to 1000 to increase model capacity. Reduce `reg_lambda` to 0.005 and `reg_alpha` to 0 to significantly lower the penalty for non-zero predictions. Lower `learning_rate` to 0.06 to allow for more stable convergence with higher capacity.
2.  **Target Clipping**: Increase `CLIP_PERCENTILE` to 98. This preserves more variance in the training targets, which provides stronger gradients and encourages the model to predict larger moves rather than defaulting to the mean.
3.  **Feature Engineering**: 
    - Added `log_vol` to handle the log-normal distribution of volume.
    - Added `vol_change_5` to capture shifts in volatility regimes.
    - Added `vol_skew_20` to capture asymmetric risk in price returns.
    - Added `tail_ratio` to distinguish between trending bars and range-bound "wicky" bars.
    - Added a normalized `vpt_20` (Volume Price Trend) to better capture volume-weighted momentum.
</DESCRIPTION>
