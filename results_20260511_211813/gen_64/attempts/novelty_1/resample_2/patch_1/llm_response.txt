The model is still experiencing partial prediction collapse (pred_std_ratio = 0.11, targeted > 0.15). To address this, I am increasing the model's expressivity by increasing `max_depth` to 7 and `n_estimators` to 1000, while significantly lowering the `learning_rate` to 0.02 to allow for a more nuanced ensemble. 

On the feature engineering side, I am adding "Exhaustion" features that measure the price distance from 24-hour highs and lows normalized by ATR. These features help identify overextended regimes where mean reversion is likely. I am also adding a 'Volatility of Volatility' (V-Vol) metric and 'Money Flow Index' (MFI) to better capture the relationship between price pressure and volume, which are critical for out-of-sample directional accuracy in 15-minute crypto futures.
