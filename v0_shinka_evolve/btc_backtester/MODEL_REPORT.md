# Technical Report: Calibrated XGBoost Alpha Model (v0)

This report details the architecture, feature engineering, and performance metrics of the refined XGBoost regression model designed to predict 15-minute Bitcoin price ratios.

## 1. Model Architecture

- **Algorithm**: XGBoost (Extreme Gradient Boosting) Regressor.
- **Objective**: `reg:squarederror` (Mean Squared Error).
- **Target Transformation**: Instead of predicting the raw ratio ($Price_{t+1} / Price_t$), the model predicts the **Basis Points (BPS) change**:
  $$y_{target} = \left(\frac{Price_{t+1}}{Price_t} - 1.0\right) \times 10,000$$
  This improves numerical stability and gradient sensitivity for the small percentage moves typical in 15-minute windows.
- **Calibration**: A post-processing variance-scaling factor ($1.0411$) is applied to correct the "regression to the mean" bias, ensuring the predicted volatility matches historical market volatility.

## 2. Feature Engineering (57 Features)

The model utilizes a "Kitchen Sink" feature set, categorized as follows:

### A. Orderbook Microstructure (Mandatory OBI)
- **OBI ($\tau \in \{1, 3, 5, 10\}$)**: Exponentially-decayed weighted imbalance across top 20 levels.
- **Spread BPS**: (Ask1 - Bid1) / Mid * 10,000.
- **Depth Ratios (5, 10)**: Volume ratios at various depths.
- **Book Pressure**: Volume ratio of levels 2-4.
- **Kyle's Lambda Est**: Proxy for price impact ($\Delta P / \Delta V$).

### B. Price Action & Momentum
- **Log Returns**: `ret_1`, `ret_3`, `ret_6`, `ret_12`, `ret_48`.
- **Volatility**: `vol_5`, `vol_20`, `vol_60` (Rolling Std).
- **Technical Indicators**: RSI (6, 14), MACD Signal, Bollinger Band % Position, ATR (14).
- **Bar Microstructure**: Momentum ratio, Wick ratios (Up/Down), Volume-to-MA ratios (5, 20).
- **Advanced**: VWAP Deviation, Autocorrelation (lag 5), Skewness (20), Kurtosis (20).

### C. Macro & Market Structure
- **Funding**: Current Funding Rate, 8-hour MA of Funding.
- **Liquidity**: Liquidation Imbalance, Buy/Sell Liquidations (1h).
- **Open Interest**: OI Change (1, 6 bars), OI-to-Volume ratio.
- **Basis**: Futures-to-Spot basis %.

### D. Time & Session
- **Cyclical Encoding**: `hour_sin`, `hour_cos`, `dow_sin`, `dow_cos`.
- **Session Flags**: `is_asia_session`, `is_us_session`, `is_weekend`.
- **Funding Timer**: `minutes_to_funding`.

## 3. Feature Importance

The top features driving the model's predictions (based on **Gain**) include:

1. **`spread_bps`**: High sensitivity to immediate liquidity gaps.
2. **`gap_open`**: Captures overnight or inter-bar price jumps.
3. **`oi_chg_6`**: Reflects mid-term positioning changes.
4. **`kyle_lambda_est`**: Indicates market "thinness" and impact potential.
5. **`funding_rate`**: Captures directional bias in perpetual swaps.
