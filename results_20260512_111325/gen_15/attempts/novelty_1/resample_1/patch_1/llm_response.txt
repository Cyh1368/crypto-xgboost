<DESCRIPTION>
This edit targets the main failure mode: the model’s signal collapses in the validation regime because it is learning overly specific level-based patterns and is too constrained to express a stable slope.

I’m making two coordinated changes:

1. Hyperparameters:
   - Relax regularization a bit so XGBoost can fit stronger but still controlled structure.
   - Increase tree count and depth slightly to recover predictive slope.
   - Lower learning rate and raise early stopping patience to let the model find more robust patterns without overfitting sharply to the training regime.
   - Keep min_child_weight above the current minimum to avoid noisy splits, but reduce it from 5 to 2 to improve expressivity.

2. Features:
   - Shift emphasis toward short lookbacks and regime-normalized signals.
   - Add rolling z-score versions of returns and volatility, which makes features more comparable across volatility regimes.
   - Add trend-of-regime features such as vol_trend, efficiency_trend, and short-horizon momentum normalized by volatility.
   - Add a multiplicative interaction between short momentum and volatility regime, which often generalizes better than absolute momentum alone.
   - Replace some longer-window dependence with shorter windows to reduce regime fragility.

The goal is to increase val slope and pred_std_ratio without sacrificing too much DA or widening the train→val gap.
</DESCRIPTION>
